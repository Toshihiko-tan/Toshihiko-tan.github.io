I'm a PhD student in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics at the University of Southern California, where I work with Prof. Doc Edge and Prof. Charleston Chiang. I'm interested in population genetics, quantitative genetics and machine learning.

Before USC, I studied at the University of Michigan, earning a B.S. in Data Science and Statistics (with Honors in Data Science) and an M.S. in Biostatistics. There I worked with Prof. Jonathan Terhorst on demographic inference, co-creating the [demestats](https://github.com/jthlab/demestats) library, and with Prof. Irina Gaynanova on methods and software for continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data, including zero-shot forecasting with large language models.
