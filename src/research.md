My research interests are in population genetics, quantitative genetics and machine learning. At USC, I work with Prof. Doc Edge and Prof. Charleston Chiang.

At the University of Michigan, I worked with Prof. Jonathan Terhorst on inferring complex demographic models from large allele frequency spectra, and with Prof. Irina Gaynanova on statistical and machine-learning methods for continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data.
